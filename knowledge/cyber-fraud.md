# Online fraud and fake relief schemes

Source: https://cybercrime.gov.in
Summary of public cyber-safety advice in India. Verify against the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

## Signs of a fake scheme or relief offer
- The link isn't on an official government domain (official sites end in .gov.in or .nic.in).
- It asks for an OTP, UPI PIN, card details or net-banking password. Government schemes never ask for these through a link.
- It creates urgency ("register within 24 hours") or asks you to forward it to many groups.
- It promises money to everyone for little or nothing.

## What to do
- Don't open the link or enter any details. Don't forward it.
- If money or an OTP was shared, call the bank immediately to block the account or card.
- Report it: call the national cybercrime helpline 1930, or file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in.
- Warn family and group members who may have received the same message.
