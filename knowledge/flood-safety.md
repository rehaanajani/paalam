# Flood safety: what to do

Source: https://ndma.gov.in
Summary of widely published flood safety advice (NDMA and state disaster-management authorities). Verify against your state's current guidance.

## If water is entering your home
- Move people, medicines, documents and drinking water to the highest floor or the roof.
- Switch off electricity at the mains and turn off gas before water reaches sockets, if it is safe to do so.
- Don't touch electrical equipment while standing in water.
- Keep a phone charged, keep a torch, and save battery: send short messages instead of calls.
- Tell rescuers about anyone who can't walk, is elderly, pregnant, a child, disabled or depends on medicine.

## Moving through water
- Don't walk or drive through moving water. Even shallow fast water can knock a person over or move a car.
- Avoid open drains and manholes: they are invisible under flood water.
- Stay away from fallen power lines.

## Water and food
- Drink only boiled, bottled or treated water. Flood water contaminates wells and taps.
- Throw away food that touched flood water.

## Helplines in India
- 112: national emergency number.
- 108: ambulance in most states.
- 1070: state emergency operations centre (disaster control room) in many states.
- 1077: district emergency operations centre in many states.
